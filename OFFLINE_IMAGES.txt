MARKETLINK OFFLINE PRODUCT IMAGES
================================
All 110 product images are bundled locally under:
assets/img/products/

The website does not request product photographs from the internet.
The database image_url field points to each local product image.
The home-page market hero is also bundled locally as:
assets/img/market-hero.jpg

These bundled images are locally generated realistic product visuals for the demo/catalog.
If you need legally licensed third-party photographs, replace the files with downloaded assets
whose license permits your intended use. Pexels states its photos are free to use under its license:
https://www.pexels.com/license/
